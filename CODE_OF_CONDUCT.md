# Code of conduct

Reasoning contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation safe,
respectful, and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Respect differing experience, identity, background, and communication style.
- Credit work accurately and protect private or embargoed information.
- Accept moderation and correction without retaliation.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publication of another
person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect the community. Decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary disclosure about a
reporter or affected person.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer identified in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) through the contact method on their GitHub
profile. If no private channel is known, request one without describing the
incident publicly. Security-sensitive reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies in repository discussions, review, project communication,
and public representation of the project.
